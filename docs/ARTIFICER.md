# Artificer coverage

Updated 3 November 2026 from both supplied Eberron: Forge of the Artificer (2025) PDFs. The physical-book scan is the pagination reference: printed p. 8 for the class table, pp. 11–12 for the 80-spell base list, pp. 7–8 for Spellcasting and Tinker’s Magic, p. 21 for Homunculus Servant.

The class now uses the published revised rules (2024 rules family), not the UA playtest. New base-list access includes Elementalism, True Strike, Arcane Vigor, Dragon’s Breath, Circle of Power and Homunculus Servant. Mending is granted separately by Tinker’s Magic at level 1, included automatically in character books, and cannot be removed while the character is an Artificer. It does not consume one of the class’s chosen cantrips.

The app shows fixed preparation allowances from the class table (2 at level 1, 6 at 5, 9 at 9, 14 at 17, 15 at 19–20). Chosen cantrips are 2, increasing to 3 at level 10 and 4 at 14, plus Mending. Single-class slot levels unlock at character levels 1, 5, 9, 13 and 17. Long Rest changes, Intelligence casting, and the mandatory proficient tool focus are explained in the class help. Mandatory costly/consumed material components still apply.

The spellbook is a collection, not an enforced learned/prepared allowance. Preparation remains manual because subclass/feat always-prepared exceptions are not modeled. Subclass spell access and non-spell class features are outside this catalogue’s automation. Multiclassing is not implemented.

The 20 supplemental spells assigned to Artificer by Tasha’s p. 12 but absent from the new base list remain available, as do explicit Arcana Unleashed additions. This preserves earlier-book options and existing saved selections; older spell text retains its source/version note. Same-name spells always use the latest verified catalogue version. This does not claim those supplemental spells are printed in Forge’s base list.

No stored character IDs or existing selections are renamed. Decoding old Artificer libraries grants Mending while retaining valid selections and prepared flags. Switching into Artificer also grants Mending; clearing the collection retains this class-granted cantrip.

See FORGE_ADDITION.md and REPLACEMENT_AUDIT.md for Homunculus Servant versus Create Homunculus. No speculative aliases are introduced.
